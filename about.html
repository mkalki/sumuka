01OUR COMPANY

From a prodigy idea
to a trusted foundry.

Established in 2013 by Gajendra Murugan, CEO of Sumuka Steels Pvt. Ltd., the company has evolved from a small foundry into a professional steel and alloy steel casting operation.

Decades of experience, a diligent work system and expertise in our techniques, tools and equipment have helped make Sumuka Steels a trusted name in the industry. Our team works meticulously across projects to produce castings to client specifications and deliver customer satisfaction.

02OUR HISTORY & CREDENTIALS

Growth shaped
by ambition and expertise.

Each stage of our journey has added capability, capacity and confidence to the work we do.

2013

A foundry begins

Sumuka Steels was established with 175 kW and 300 kg crucible furnaces.

2017

Capacity expands

The foundry added 250 kW and 500 kg crucible furnaces. The year also marked the company’s highest sales rate and IBR foundry recognition.

Today

Built to deliver

Sumuka Steels has a total foundry capacity of 150 MT per month and more than 600 development patterns. Our modern manufacturing complex brings pattern shop, moulding, melting, fettling, heat treatment, shot blasting and testing together.
